CASE STUDY

An AgriTech platform connecting farmers with buyers to reduce post-harvest losses.

1st prize · TCC Hack & Defend 2026NanaTech 2026Startup Loop

The problem

Smallholder vegetable farmers lose part of their harvest because they cannot find buyers in time. Perishable produce spoils, and with it part of the family income.

The solution

Farmers post their harvest in under a minute: product, quantity, location and urgency. Buyers find offers on a map and contact the farmer directly on WhatsApp.

Users & value proposition

  • Smallholder vegetable farmers
  • Buyers: households, restaurants, traders
  • Less waste, more income, fresh produce that is easier to get

My role

Founder. I have carried AgriFlash on my own from the start: field research, design, development, pitching, social media and business model.

Journey

  • 2023 — at CDEJ Béthel, I watch tomatoes, peppers and lettuce go to waste for lack of buyers.
  • July 4, 2026 — 1st prize at TCC Hack & Defend with a web prototype.
  • Aug–Oct 2026 — Startup Loop · Innov'Up program.
  • September 2026 — selected for NanaTech, building the MVP.
  • October 3, 2026 — NanaTech UX/UI module: user journeys and architecture.

Prototype & tech choices

  • Prototype: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet.js, Chart.js, GitHub Pages
  • MVP: React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL
  • Designed for phones and low-bandwidth connections
