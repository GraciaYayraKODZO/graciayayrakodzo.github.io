CASE STUDY

AcadPro

A web-based school management solution for private schools. My role: getting the word out and booking demos.

Communications & outreach60+ schools targetedLaunched Sept 18, 2026

The project

AcadPro brings school management into one place: enrollment and admissions, student and teacher records, grades and report cards, attendance, timetables, fees and communication with parents. The project is supervised by Mr. Dodji EDOU.

My role

In charge of communications and outreach to private schools in Lomé.

What I did

  • Built a database of more than 60 private schools in Lomé, shared with the team
  • Designed a two-step approach: email first, then a phone call for schools we only had a number for
  • Wrote the email templates, approved by the supervisor on September 18, 2026
  • Launched the campaign on September 18, 2026, at the start of the school year
  • Present the solution to interested schools

Key takeaway

A good product is not enough: you have to present it simply, at the right time, to the right person. The first email has a single goal: booking a demo.
